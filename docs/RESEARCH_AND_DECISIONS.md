# Research and product decisions

## Research standard
Prioritise primary sources: NASSCOM GCC reports, Invest India, official state policy portals, company announcements and annual reports. Reputable business journalism is useful for discovery, but claims should link to the original source wherever possible. Provider case studies must be labelled provider-reported.

## Evidence labels
- **Verified fact:** directly supported by a named source; preserve source URL and date.
- **Reported claim:** attributed to a company, publisher, or provider; not independently audited.
- **Estimate:** methodology, scope, date and uncertainty disclosed.
- **Hypothesis:** an assumption to validate; never presented as an observed fact.
- **Illustrative:** sample content used only to demonstrate a workflow.

## Non-negotiable data rules
1. News article counts are not GCC counts.
2. Do not infer talent supply, compensation, office rent, or incentive savings from news mentions.
3. Deduplicate syndicated or repeated coverage where possible.
4. Show publication date and retrieval date when available.
5. Keep contradictory claims visible and explain scope/date differences instead of selecting the most convenient number.
6. A provider's advertised services are not proof of successful delivery.
7. Never fabricate interviews, customer quotes, survey results, market-size estimates, company logos, or provider performance records.

## MVP prioritisation rationale
Start with a repeatable city-comparison workflow combining source-linked signals and focused AI synthesis. It offers an understandable first job, draws on discoverable public information, and lets the product demonstrate traceability. Defer high-maintenance datasets (salary, office inventory, incentive eligibility) until source coverage, licensing, update cadence, and user demand are validated.

The opportunity ratings and scoring weights in the assignment deck are product judgments, not user-research findings.

## Validation backlog
- Interview strategy / site-selection users about their last city shortlist.
- Ask which source types are trusted, which conflicts are common, and who must approve the decision.
- Test whether cited synthesis beats their current spreadsheet workflow.
- Validate recurring use and budget ownership before pricing.
- Audit source freshness, duplicates, and claim-level citation coverage.
